# Music work for Shoot 'em (2026-11-14 to 15)

How the soundtrack that replaced BadLands and boombass was chosen and made.
SHORTLIST.md has every candidate, its licence and source, and David's
decisions (search for "David's decisions"). Paths in it point at the old
scratch folder; the same files are here:

- `remixes/`: every remix and extended mix made (.xm/.it), approved and not.
  The approved ones are copied into `assets/music/`.
- `sources/`: the original modules they were made from (Drozerix public
  domain, fuzion_mixer CC BY 4.0, Pip Malt CC BY, m0d public domain) and
  Juhani Junkala's CC0 pack notes (`junkala/INFO.txt`). The pack itself,
  `junkala/action.zip` (5 Chiptunes (Action), OpenGameArt), is kept locally
  but not in git.
- `tools/`: the Python scripts that built the remixes (XM/IT writing,
  synthesised drums, renders through xmrs, the game's player).
- `previews/`: WAV renders David listened to (local only, not in git).
