# Permission

Shoot 'em is Jan Ringoš' freeware game (2005). Its freeware licence
allowed only unchanged copies, and its source is lost, so this edition
exists by his permission, given by email to David Sandquist of
Asciiverse:

- **From:** Jan Ringoš <jan.ringos@trimcore.cz> (Trim Core Software s.r.o.)
- **Date:** 13 November 2026, 21:48 (Central European Time)
- **Subject:** Re: Shoot 'em in today's terminals

> Feel free to port the game in any way you like; even decompile it if
> necessary.
> Also consider the permission to publish it in any way, or in any
> derivative form, granted in advance.
>
> The reason is: I can't find the source code anywhere.
> Also check the .TXT files in the ZIP
> (https://tringi.trimcore.cz/Shoot_em_ASCII), there are some
> interesting options you may want to offer to players. E.g. different
> audio or nicer 4×MSAA mode.
>
> I'm looking forward to see if you manage to crack it and have it
> playable.

The game's design, its sounds and its look are his. The two music
modules he shipped with it are "boombass" by The Kid and "BadLands" by
Matt Furniss, as named inside the modules.
